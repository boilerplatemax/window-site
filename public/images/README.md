## Recommended specs
- **Format:** JPG (80–85% quality) or WebP
- **Hero images:** minimum 1920 × 1080 px, landscape
- **Gallery images:** minimum 1400 × 900 px
- **File size:** keep under 500 KB per image (compress with Squoosh or similar)
